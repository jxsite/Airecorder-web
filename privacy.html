Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our users. This includes:

  • Account Information: When you register or log in, we collect your email address and authentication credentials.
  • Voice & Transcription Data: We temporarily upload your voice recordings to our secure servers to process transcription and AI summarization. These audio files and transcripts are securely stored in your personal account database.
  • Device Information: We may collect device identifiers (like hardware models and OS versions) to prevent abuse and manage trial limits.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our AI voice transcription and structured summary services.
  • To manage your VIP subscription status and track fair usage limits.
  • To respond to user suggestions and support requests submitted through the feedback feature.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with third-party service providers necessary to run our service (such as Google Gemini APIs for voice-to-text / text summarization, and Creem.io for payment and billing processing) under strict security guidelines.

4. Data Security

We implement robust industrial-standard security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions and restricted database access, to protect your recordings and personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights & Choice

You can delete your recorded notes and audio files at any time within the app. If you wish to delete your entire cloud account and associated data permanently, please contact us at our customer support email.
